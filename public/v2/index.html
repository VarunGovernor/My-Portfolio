ABOUT — THE HUMAN BEHIND THE TERMINAL

About, minus
the buzzword
smoothie.

No stock portrait. No floating initials. Just someone who enjoys making computers do useful things.

I'm a full-stack engineer, AI researcher and developer who enjoys turning complicated ideas into things people can actually use. I like ambitious projects, but I like a working product even more.

I work across web and mobile applications, data-heavy systems, automation, and the awkward bits of engineering that aren't nearly as glamorous in a demo. Architecture, integrations, debugging—yes, that part where one tiny bug turns into a three-hour detective story.

I'm genuinely curious about AI, especially where it can make workflows smarter or remove unnecessary work. I don't believe every button needs an AI label slapped on it. Sometimes the clever solution is a simpler form, a faster database query, or knowing when not to add another dependency.

I care about clear interfaces, maintainable code, honest communication and shipping something that's reliable after the exciting launch-day screenshots. My favorite kind of project is the one where I get to learn, experiment, break something in testing, fix it, and then see someone actually benefit from it.

No ninja title. No guru cape. Just thoughtful systems—and an unreasonable number of browser tabs.
